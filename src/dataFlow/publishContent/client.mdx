## Stay connected offline with secure SMS messaging

**RelaySMS (Swob)** empowers you to send encrypted online messages via SMS — even when you're offline.  
Designed with Android and iOS users in mind, this tool securely stores your OAuth2 tokens (for services like **Gmail**, **Twitter**, and **Telegram**) in encrypted Vaults, allowing you to interact with online services without an internet connection.

Whether you're in a low-signal area or conserving data, **RelaySMS** keeps your communication flowing—**safely and reliably.**

---

### Android

RelaySMS for Android offers a seamless way to interact with online services without internet access.  
Once configured, it securely sends your messages over SMS by leveraging encrypted Vaults that store your OAuth2 tokens.  
Tweet, email, or send Telegram messages from remote locations—**your data stays private and your communication uninterrupted**.

### iOS

RelaySMS for iOS brings the same offline connectivity to Apple users.  
With token-based authorization and end-to-end encryption, RelaySMS lets you send secure SMS-based commands to services like Gmail, Twitter, and Telegram—**without needing mobile data or Wi-Fi**.
