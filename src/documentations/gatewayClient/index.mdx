RelaySMS relies on **gateway clients** to route and relay SMS-based messages to online services.  
Each gateway client is identified by its **MSISDN (Mobile Station International Subscriber Directory Number)** and acts as a trusted relay node in your communication network.

---

### What are Gateway Clients?

A gateway client is a registered phone number that acts as a relay for sending and receiving messages on behalf of other users. This allows devices without internet access to interact with online services by passing messages through these always-on gateway clients.

RelaySMS uses these clients to:

- Receive SMS messages with embedded commands or data
- Process and forward the message to the appropriate cloud service (e.g., Gmail, Twitter, Telegram)
- Return responses to the sender via SMS

---

### Managing Gateway Clients

From within the RelaySMS app, you can:

- **View** your list of registered gateway clients
- **Add** new gateway clients by entering a valid MSISDN
- **Switch** between clients depending on your location or preferred route

This ensures flexibility in managing your message routing, especially when operating across different regions or carriers.
